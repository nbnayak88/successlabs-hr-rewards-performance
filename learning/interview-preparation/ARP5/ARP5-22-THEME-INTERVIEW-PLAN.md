# ARP5 — 22-Theme Interview Preparation Plan

## Applied SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay

**Scope:** Compensation & Variable Pay as an enterprise rewards capability.

**Transformation spine:**
Employee → Eligibility → Compensation Planning → Guidelines → Recommendations → Calibration → Approval → Award → Variable Pay → Payroll/Finance Alignment → Total Rewards Outcome

**Boundary**
- ARP5 owns Compensation & Variable Pay, compensation planning, worksheets, guidelines, recommendations, eligibility, merit/bonus planning, approvals, award outcomes and rewards insights.
- APH3 owns Performance & Goals.
- AGL4 owns Succession & Development.
- AWF1 owns Employee Central.
- APE7 owns Employee Central Payroll.
