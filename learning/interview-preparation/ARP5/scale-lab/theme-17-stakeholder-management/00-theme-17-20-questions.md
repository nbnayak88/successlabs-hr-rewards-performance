# 20 Scenario-Based Interview Questions & STAR Answers

**Stream:** ARP5 — Applied SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay  
**Theme:** 17 — Stakeholder Management  
**Target:** 20 unique scenario-based interview questions  
**Answer method:** STAR — Situation → Task → Action → Result  
**Product posture:** HCM-first, Compensation & Variable Pay, stakeholder-led and architecture-aware

## Purpose

This theme tests whether the candidate can align HR, business leaders, managers, Finance, Payroll, IT, Security, Data, vendors, and implementation teams around Compensation outcomes. The emphasis is on conflicting priorities, decision rights, executive alignment, local-versus-global interests, escalation, change adoption, governance, and measurable stakeholder outcomes.

---

### HR-ARP5-B17-Q01
### Interview Question
How would you align HR and Finance when they disagree on the Compensation budget?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** HR wanted greater manager flexibility while Finance required a strict enterprise budget.  
**Task:** I needed to create a decision that both functions could support.  
**Action:** I separated policy objectives from implementation preferences, quantified the financial impact of alternatives, clarified decision rights, and proposed governed flexibility through thresholds and approvals.  
**Result:** Both functions aligned around a controlled operating model.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example
I would use budgets, guidelines, manager visibility, approval thresholds, and exception governance to translate the agreed policy into Compensation.

### SME Probe
Who should own the final budget policy decision?

**Task:** I needed to measure stakeholder effectiveness rather than activity.  
**Action:** I tracked decision turnaround time, unresolved decisions, rework, escalation frequency, requirement churn, stakeholder readiness, adoption, and satisfaction.
