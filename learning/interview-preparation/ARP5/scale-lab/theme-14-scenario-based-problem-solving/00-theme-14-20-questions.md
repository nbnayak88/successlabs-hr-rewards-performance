# 20 Scenario-Based Interview Questions & STAR Answers

**Stream:** ARP5 — Applied SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay  
**Theme:** 14 — Scenario-Based Problem Solving  
**Target:** 20 unique scenario-based interview questions  
**Answer method:** STAR — Situation → Task → Action → Result  
**Product posture:** HCM-first, Compensation & Variable Pay, scenario-led and architecture-aware

## Purpose

This theme tests whether the candidate can reason through ambiguous Compensation situations, make sound decisions under constraints, balance business and technology considerations, and produce measurable outcomes. The emphasis is on structured problem solving rather than pure troubleshooting or root-cause mechanics.

### HR-ARP5-B14-Q01
### Interview Question
The business wants to launch a global merit cycle in six weeks, but requirements are still changing. What would you do?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A global organization had a fixed compensation-cycle deadline while business stakeholders continued adding requirements.  
**Task:** I needed to protect the launch date without accepting uncontrolled scope.  
**Action:** I separated mandatory policy requirements from enhancements, established decision owners, prioritized the minimum viable cycle, froze critical design decisions, and placed lower-priority items into a controlled backlog.  
**Result:** The organization retained the committed launch while preserving a roadmap for future improvements.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example
I would prioritize core eligibility, components, guidelines, budgets, worksheets, workflow, approvals, and required integrations before optional experience enhancements.

### SME Probe
How do you decide what is truly mandatory for a Compensation go-live?
