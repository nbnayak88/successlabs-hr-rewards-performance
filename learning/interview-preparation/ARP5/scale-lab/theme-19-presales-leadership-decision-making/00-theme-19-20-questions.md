**Stream:** ARP5 — Applied SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay  
**Theme:** 19 — Presales / Leadership / Decision Making  
**Target:** 20 unique scenario-based interview questions  
**Answer method:** STAR — Situation → Task → Action → Result  
**Product posture:** HCM-first, Compensation & Variable Pay, leadership-led and architecture-aware

## Purpose

This theme tests whether the candidate can lead Compensation decisions, shape solution strategy, support presales, evaluate alternatives, manage executive trade-offs, build business cases, lead teams, and make accountable decisions under uncertainty.

### HR-ARP5-B19-Q01
### Interview Question
How would you lead a presales conversation for a global SAP SuccessFactors Compensation transformation?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A global organization wanted to modernize a fragmented annual compensation process.  
**Task:** I needed to shape a credible solution and business case rather than deliver a product demonstration.  
**Action:** I clarified business outcomes, current pain points, policy, process, data, integration, security, user experience, operating model, scope, risks, and transformation roadmap. I then mapped the needs to a pragmatic target architecture.  
**Result:** The client received a transformation proposition tied to measurable business outcomes rather than a feature catalogue.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example
I would demonstrate the end-to-end journey from eligibility and planning through recommendations, approval, award, Variable Pay, and downstream alignment.

### SME Probe
What should you discover before demonstrating the product?

### HR-ARP5-B19-Q02
### Interview Question

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A prospect wanted a rapid commercial proposal with limited discovery information.  
**Task:** I needed to provide useful direction without making unsupported commitments.  
**Action:** I stated assumptions, identified known and unknown scope, proposed a short discovery phase, defined solution options, and separated indicative estimates from committed estimates.  
**Result:** The prospect received a useful proposal while commercial and delivery risk remained transparent.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example
I would identify expected populations, countries, templates, Compensation and Variable Pay scope, integrations, migration needs, security, testing, and rollout assumptions before firm estimates.
