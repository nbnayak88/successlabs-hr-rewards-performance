# OmniVerse Energy Limited — Compensation & Variable Pay Foundation Sprint

**Programme:** Super Sprint 20™ | **Sector:** Energy | **Platform:** SAP SuccessFactors | **Lab:** SAL — Strategy & Architecture Lab (Design) | **Architecture stream:** Applied Application Architecture | **Progression:** Pre-Foundation → Foundation | **Duration:** 20 hours (10 × 2 hours)

> This is a fictional learning case study. All employee names, salaries, percentages and operating assumptions are illustrative, not verified client facts.

## Purpose
Build beginner-level understanding of compensation and variable pay before moving into SAP configuration. The objective is **Foundation-level literacy and solution-design participation**, not expert implementation proficiency.

## Foundational concepts
- **Fixed compensation:** base salary and fixed allowances.
- **Total rewards:** cash compensation, benefits and other employer rewards.
- **Merit increase:** an approved change to fixed salary, usually during a salary review.
- **Variable Pay:** conditional bonus or incentive based on eligibility, targets, goals and plan rules.
- **Target bonus:** an opportunity rather than a guaranteed payout.
- **Employee Central:** employee/job/pay data foundation; downstream payroll processing requires approved integration.

## Session 02 — Persona catalogue (illustrative)
| Persona | Annual fixed pay | Target bonus | Energy-sector reward considerations |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Plant Technician | ₹6,00,000 | 8% | Shift/site allowance; overtime eligibility |
| Electrical Engineer | ₹12,00,000 | 10% | Technical skills, travel, merit vs promotion |
| Field Maintenance Lead | ₹18,00,000 | 15% | Remote-site and hardship allowances |
| Plant Operations Manager | ₹36,00,000 | 20% | Operational scorecard and leadership benefits |
| Regional Energy Executive | ₹80,00,000 | 35% | Executive governance and bonus caps |

**Example salary review:** ₹12,00,000 × 8% = ₹96,000 increase; new annual fixed salary ₹12,96,000.

**Example bonus:** ₹1,20,000 target × (40% × 110% + 35% × 100% + 25% × 120%) = **₹1,30,800**. This is a simplified linear teaching example, not a confirmed SAP plan formula. Real plans require eligibility, proration, thresholds, caps, funding, approvals and accurate data. Safety goals must avoid discouraging incident reporting.

## Solution-design checkpoints
1. Identify reward policies, legal entities, countries, currencies, pay grades and eligible populations.
2. Confirm employee/job/pay sources and effective-dated data quality in Employee Central.
3. Define compensation worksheets, guidelines, budgets, permissions and approval routes.
4. Define bonus plan eligibility, business goals, weights, employee history and payout rules.
5. Trace approved results into employee records and payroll integrations; reconcile exceptions.
6. Validate fairness, security, auditability and unintended safety incentives.

## Assessment and progression
After each session: five-minute review, worked-example validation and teach-back using the Feynman technique. Final evaluation: ten progressively harder questions (recall → application → design) and an OmniVerse capstone presentation. Advance to Foundation when the learner can explain both modules, perform basic calculations and trace the end-to-end architecture without relying on memorized terms.
